## What these checks do not establish

- **Comparable tuning budget** is met by tuning *nothing*: every method uses the original repository's hyper-parameters. That makes the comparison equal-effort, but not equal-optimum. A tuned centralized model may close the gap (report §10.1, §10.6).
- **Five seeds** show consistent orderings but cannot support significance tests at α = 0.05; none is claimed.
- **Wall-clock times** of 12 runs are invalid (suspend / concurrent GPU use) and are flagged; runtime conclusions use the dedicated benchmark in `results/v2_timing/`.
- **Not run:** the optional `local_dirichlet` experiments (not part of the four Phase-2 requirements).
- These checks verify that the experiments were executed as specified. Whether the interpretation is right is argued in `EXPERIMENT_REPORT.md` §10 and `CONCLUSION_CHECK.md`.
